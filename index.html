How This Queue Works

1. Check in players to put them at the end of the waiting line.
2. Start a doubles match with the first four people in line.
3. Enter the score (optional), then finish the match.
4. If someone gets injured, choose Replace player on their court. Their current game is not credited to them.
5. All four finishing players rotate to the back of the line. If auto-fill is on, the next group starts automatically.
